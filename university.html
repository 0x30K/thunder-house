Dean's Convocation Address

Why Thunder University Exists

Welcome to Thunder University. Today’s music is consumed at lightning speed—an endless feed of algorithmic soundbites, viral TikTok hooks, and fleeting memes. You can know every trending snippet without ever knowing the name of the genius who spent 90 days in a room agonizing over the bassline.

Real power in music comes from lineage. Knowing that Michael Jackson’s dance mechanics trace directly back to James Brown; that Bruno Mars spent 15 years as an Elvis impersonator and uncredited songwriter before hitting #1; that Pharrell Williams learned syncopated jazz rhythm on a high school marching band snare drum; that Kanye West chopped vintage Chaka Khan soul records on a beat machine with his jaw wired shut; and that Juice WRLD took classical childhood piano training and invented a new genre of non-stop freestyle emo-trap.

This academy is your compass. Dive deep, listen with intention, and graduate into a true connoisseur of sound.
