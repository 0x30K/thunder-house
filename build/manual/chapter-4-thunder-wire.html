Chapter 04

Thunder Wire & Morning Show Broadcasting Intelligence

Thunder Wire is our real-time entertainment journalism and morning show production suite. It powers our daily #WAKEITUP morning broadcast, keeping our DJs culturally sharp, informed, and ready to host high-engagement live streams.

1. The 5 Morning Show Story Blocks

A professional morning radio or video broadcast cannot be disorganized banter. Thunder Wire automatically ingests, deduplicates, and categorizes trending entertainment news into 5 structured show segments:

Zoom In
Figure 4.1 The Thunder Wire operational suite at admin/wire.html. Displays real-time ingested stories filtered by category, virality score, and verification status.
  1. The Lead Story: Major headline industry news—groundbreaking festival announcements, record label shifts, historic Billboard milestones, and stadium tour announcements.
  2. The Shade: Pop culture debates, playful social media drama, celebrity feuds, and viral TikTok trends that drive caller discussion.
  3. Fresh Drops: Album reviews, surprise midnight EP drops, standout music videos, and newly signed underground talent.
  4. Wild World: Bizarre, humorous, and unbelievable news stories from across the globe that create laughter and comedic relief on air.
  5. Real Talk: Motivational community topics, mental health in the creative industry, financial literacy for artists, and local Oklahoma City spotlights.

2. The Serverless Ingestion Engine

Behind the scenes, our Vercel serverless function (located at /api/wire-scan) continuously monitors high-authority industry RSS feeds including XXL Magazine, Billboard, AllHipHop, and HipHopDX:

  • Direct Server-Side Ingestion: By fetching feeds through our backend API, the system bypasses browser CORS restrictions and parses RSS/XML feeds in fractions of a second.
  • Deduplication In-Memory: Stories covered by multiple outlets are deduplicated by target URL so our morning show dashboard remains clean and uncluttered.
  • Virality & Verification Tagging: Stories are indexed with an estimated Virality Score (1 to 100) and flagged as confirmed, developing, or rumor so hosts know how to frame the topic on air.

3. Live OBS Lower-Thirds Broadcast Overlay

When broadcasting live to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook Live, or TikTok, hosts do not need to create manual graphics in Photoshop. Thunder House ENT includes a live, transparent browser source overlay located at thunderhouseent.com/wire-overlay.html (or /obs).

Zoom In
Figure 4.2 The live OBS graphic overlay. Displays dynamic lower-third news tickers, host questions, and show block badges with a transparent background.
How to Add to OBS Studio

Setting up the overlay in OBS Studio takes less than 30 seconds:

  1. In OBS Studio, click the + under Sources and select Browser.
  2. Set the URL to https://thunderhouseent.com/wire-overlay.html (or http://localhost:8080/wire-overlay.html for local testing).
  3. Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080. Check "Shutdown source when not visible".
  4. Click OK. As the host advances stories in admin/wire.html, the lower-third graphic automatically animates in real time!
