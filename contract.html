This Entertainment Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of the date of execution by and between Thunder House Entertainment LLC ("DJ / Company"), and the individual(s) or organization specified above ("Client").

01. Scope of Services & Performance Schedule

Company agrees to provide professional disc jockey (DJ), master of ceremonies (MC), and sound reproduction services for Client's event. Load-in and setup shall commence approximately ninety (90) minutes prior to guest arrival. Sound checks and system calibration will conclude prior to the scheduled event start time.

Agreed Showtime Hours: 5:00 PM to 11:00 PM on November 20, 2026. Additional overtime requested by Client on-site shall be billed at $150.00 per hour (or fraction thereof), subject to venue curfew and DJ availability.

02. Compensation, Date Retainer & Balance Schedule

Base Entertainment Package $0.00

Retainer: The Initial Date-Lock Retainer is non-refundable and constitutes reservation of the specific date, pre-production planning, and mutual commitment. The remaining balance is due seven (7) business days prior to showtime.

03. Venue Environment, Power & Weather Protection

Client is responsible for coordinating facility access and technical power provisions with the designated venue:

  • Electrical Power: Client guarantees one (1) dedicated 120-volt, 20-amp grounded AC electrical circuit located within twenty-five (25) feet of the DJ booth setup area.
  • Outdoor Events & Shelter: If any portion of the performance is held outdoors, Client must provide a waterproof, overhead covered shelter with three (3) enclosed sides (e.g. canopy tent or pavilion) to protect sensitive computer, lighting, and audio equipment from direct sunlight, thermal overheating, high winds, and moisture/rain.
  • Inclement Weather: DJ reserves the right to suspend or cease operation if electrical equipment is threatened by precipitation, condensation, or direct heat until safe shelter is provided. No refunds are issued for weather interruptions.

04. Equipment Protection & Guest Conduct Indemnity

Client acknowledges that all equipment, including sound systems, DJ controllers, microphones, lighting fixtures, and special effects units (cold sparks/fog), represent high-value professional assets. Client assumes full financial liability for any damage, loss, theft, or liquid spills caused to Company's equipment by event attendees, guests, or venue staff during the contracted period.

05. Cancellation & Rescheduling Terms

Should Client cancel this Agreement more than thirty (30) days prior to the event, the initial retainer shall be retained by Company as liquidated damages and all further obligations cease. If Client cancels within thirty (30) days of the event, the full remaining contract balance shall become immediately due and payable, acknowledging that Company has turned down other potential bookings for this date.

In the event of an unavoidable emergency requiring date rescheduling, Company will apply 100% of the paid retainer to any mutually open date within twelve (12) calendar months of the original event date.

06. Performance Roster & Backup DJ Guarantee

Thunder House ENT operates a certified, vetted DJ roster. In the extraordinary and unforeseen event of severe illness, accident, or hospitalization of the assigned lead DJ, Company guarantees a replacement DJ of equal or higher experience from its certified staff. All music preferences, must-play tracks, and timeline instructions stored in the VIP Client Portal shall transfer seamlessly to ensure an uninterrupted performance.

07. Media Release for Promotional Showcase

Client grants Company permission to capture brief video clips and photographs of the dance floor, lighting, and crowd atmosphere for promotional portfolio and social media use. No guest names or sensitive identifying details will be published without prior consent.

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Oklahoma.
