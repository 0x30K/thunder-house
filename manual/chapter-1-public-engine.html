3. The 72-Hour Date Hold Rule

To avoid double-booking while giving prospective clients adequate time to review paperwork without feeling high-pressure sales tactics, our platform implements the **72-Hour Calendar Hold Mechanism**.

Zoom In
Figure 1.2 The official Event Reservation Intake form. Upon submission, the system stamps the record with a timestamp and calculates an expiration exactly 72 hours in the future.
Business Rule: Hold Expiration Logic

When a client submits the intake form, their selected performance date in public.thunderhouse_availability is flagged as status = 'quote_held'. This blocks any other client from reserving that date on the website.

However, a hold is not a confirmed booking. The client has exactly 72 hours to pay the $200 retainer fee. If 72 hours elapse without a payment, our automated background process (quote_hold_releaser) automatically releases the date back to open availability.

Why 72 Hours?

  • Prevents Calendar Paralysis: Unqualified leads cannot indefinitely freeze high-demand Saturday wedding dates without financial commitment.
  • Creates Healthy Urgency: Prospective clients recognize that our dates are in high demand across the Oklahoma City metro, encouraging them to sign contracts promptly.
  • Automated Courtesy Reminders: At the 48-hour mark (24 hours before expiration), the system dispatches an automated courtesy reminder email to the client warning them that their calendar hold will release the following morning if the retainer is unpaid.

4. Live Visual Editor Mode for Superusers

Superusers can edit public website text, phone numbers, about copy, and service details directly on the live site without editing HTML source files:

  1. Ensure you are logged into your admin account.
  2. Navigate to the homepage with the live edit parameter: thunderhouseent.com/index.html?edit=1.
  3. Hover over any headline or paragraph to reveal an edit pencil. Click to modify text inline.
  4. Click the floating "Save Live Website Overrides" button. The changes are saved immediately to thunderhouse_site_settings and reflected instantly for all public visitors!
