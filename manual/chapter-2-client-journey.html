Chapter 02

The Client Journey: Quotes, Contracts, Square Invoices & The Planning Portal

Once an inquiry is initiated, our transactional engine transforms interest into legally binding contracts, secure payment receipts, and collaborative event music playlists.

1. Quotes, Invoices & Square Retainer Deposits

Thunder House ENT utilizes a unified financial billing system linked directly with Square Payments. When a quote is generated, the client receives a secure URL (e.g. thunderhouseent.com/invoice.html?id=INV-8492) rendering an itemized invoice breakdown.

Zoom In
Figure 2.1 The live client invoice screen. Note the split payment architecture: the $200 date-lock retainer can be paid immediately online via credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay through Square.

The $200 Non-Refundable Retainer Protocol

In the entertainment industry, dates are irreplaceable inventory. If a client books an October Saturday and cancels the night before, our company cannot easily resell that performance slot. Therefore, every single event requires a $200 non-refundable retainer fee:

  • Applied to Total: The $200 fee is not an extra charge; it is credited directly toward the package total (e.g., on a $700 Thunder Party package, paying the $200 retainer leaves a remaining balance of $500).
  • Date Lock Trigger: The instant Square confirms authorization via webhook, our database automatically updates the date from quote_held to booked, halting the 72-hour hold countdown clock.
  • 14-Day Balance Due Date: The remaining balance is strictly due 14 days before the performance date. This ensures staff scheduling and equipment prep are completed without financial uncertainty.

2. The Digital Contract & Legal E-Signatures

No DJ steps foot on an event stage without a fully executed legal agreement. Our platform provides a paperless, browser-based contract signing experience located at thunderhouseent.com/contract.html.

Zoom In
Figure 2.2 The legal contract interface with touch-sensitive signature pad. Works on iPhones, iPads, Android tablets, and desktop mice.

Key Contract Clauses Every Staff Member Must Know:

  1. Performance Scope: Outlines the exact start and end times, venue address, and package equipment included.
  2. Safe Power & Staging: Requires the client/venue to provide standard 120V 15A or 20A grounded electrical outlets within 25 feet of the DJ booth, plus overhead shelter if the performance is outdoors.
  3. Overtime Terms: If the party is rocking and the host asks the DJ to continue playing past the contracted time, overtime is billed at $150 per additional hour (or fraction thereof), subject to DJ agreement and venue curfew.
  4. E-Signature Audit Trail: When the client signs the canvas, the system bundles their signature image, timestamp, client IP address, and browser user-agent into a cryptographic record stored permanently in public.thunderhouse_contracts.

3. The Client Planning Portal

After paying their retainer and signing their agreement, clients receive access to the Thunder House Planning Portal (thunderhouseent.com/portal.html). This portal eliminates messy back-and-forth emails by centralizing all event specifics:

Zoom In
Figure 2.3 The Client Planning Portal dashboard. Clients can adjust timelines, list bridal party names for introductions, and curate their event soundtrack.

Within the portal, clients can configure three vital categories:

  • The Music Vibe & Genre Sliders: Clients indicate their musical preferences across Hip-Hop, Top 40, R&B, 90s/2000s Throwbacks, Country, and Latin.
  • Must-Play & Do-Not-Play Lists: Clients submit their top 10 essential anthems and specify songs or artists that must never be played under any circumstances (e.g. no "Cha Cha Slide" or no specific ex-partner songs).
  • VIP Timeline Moments: For weddings, this includes prelude cues, processional songs, bride's entrance, grand entrance, first dance, father-daughter dance, mother-son dance, toast speakers, bouquet toss, and exit song.

4. Live Crowd Guest Requests Portal

During the event, clients and guests do not need to crowd around the DJ booth or interrupt the performer to request tracks. Thunder House provides a dedicated mobile web portal located at thunderhouseent.com/requests.html.

Zoom In
Figure 2.4 The live mobile crowd requests page. Guests scan a small acrylic QR placard placed at the DJ booth or table tent cards to submit requests directly from their phones.
Clean Edit & Moderation Guardrails

Guests can submit track requests and optional shoutouts. However, the system enforces strict guardrails:

  • DJ Approval Required: Requests do not automatically play. They appear in the DJ's live Run of Show queue with Approve or Decline buttons.
  • Radio / Clean Edit Enforcement: For weddings, school dances, and family birthdays, our database flags explicit versions and prompts the DJ to play only the Clean Radio Edit.
  • Do-Not-Play Filter: If a guest requests a song that the host placed on the "Do-Not-Play" list, the system flags it in red so the DJ never accidentally violates the client's wishes!
