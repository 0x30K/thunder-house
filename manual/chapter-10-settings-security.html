Chapter 10

Settings Hub, Email SMTP Relays & Disaster Recovery

Located at admin/settings.html, the Settings Hub controls company-wide operational parameters, transactional email credentials, staff security permissions, and error logging.

1. The Operations Settings Hub

The settings screen is divided into 10 specialized sections accessible via anchor subnavigation:

Zoom In
Figure 10.1 The Settings Hub at admin/settings.html. Controls booking rules, email dispatch, team permissions, and gear scanner preferences.

Key Global Parameters:

  • Hold Hours Window (Default: 72 hours): How long an online quote intake reserves the date on our calendar before releasing.
  • Retainer Deposit Amount (Default: $200): The non-refundable date-lock fee applied directly toward package totals.
  • Balance Due Window (Default: 14 days): Number of days prior to event performance date when final payment must be received.
  • Automated Review Outreach: Sends Google Review links 2 days after event execution.

2. Email SMTP Relays & Google App Passwords

All automated notifications (inquiry summaries, retainer receipts, balance reminders, and hold expiration warnings) are dispatched via our secure Gmail SMTP Relay:

  • Sending Address: olifedigital@gmail.com
  • Authentication: Uses a 16-character Google App Password stored securely in Supabase site settings.
  • Testing the Relay: Superusers can click "Send Test" in admin/settings.html at any time to verify outgoing delivery.

3. System Error Logs & Health Monitor

At the bottom of the Settings Hub sits our automated System Error Logger:

  • In-Memory Deduplication: Identical errors occurring within 60 seconds are collapsed into a single incident with an occurrence count counter to prevent log flooding.
  • Auto-Pruning: The log table (public.thunderhouse_error_logs) automatically retains only the last 200 distinct events to conserve database space.

4. Disaster Recovery Protocols for Live Gigs

The Golden Rule: Always Have Local Redundancy

Venues occasionally experience cell network blackouts or Wi-Fi failures. Every Thunder House DJ must follow our redundancy checklist:

  1. Dual USB Flash Drives: Every weekend crate from Thunder Crate must be exported to at least two high-speed USB 3.0 flash drives stored in separate road cases.
  2. Offline Master Library on E: Drive: The DJ laptop must have direct access to E:\Thunder House - Music. Never rely solely on cloud streaming during a live performance!
  3. Printed Run-of-Show: Always keep a printed or offline PDF copy of the bride's timeline, VIP moment cues, and bridal party names at the DJ booth.
