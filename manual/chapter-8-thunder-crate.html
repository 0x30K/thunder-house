Thunder Crate, Music Scout Engine & The Cloud Vault

Located at admin/music.html, Thunder Crate is our proprietary digital record pool. Powered by our automated Python discovery engine, it ingests trending weekly releases, generates Clean and Dirty audio pairs, researches artist social channels, and enables seamless mobile streaming without hogging cloud storage.

1. The Automated Music Scout Architecture

Instead of manually browsing record pools every weekend, our background Python daemon (scripts/music_scout_engine.py) wakes up automatically at 6:00 AM CST to scout trending releases across Hip-Hop, R&B, Pop, and Latin charts:

Figure 8.1 The Thunder Crate interface at admin/music.html. Shows Clean/Dirty buttons, artist research chips, release dates, and download options.

Clean & Dirty Dual-Audio Ingestion:

A hallmark of professional DJing is playing the appropriate version for the venue. Our engine downloads two distinct audio cuts for every single release:

  • Clean (Radio Edit): Explicit profanity muted or reversed. Mandatory for weddings, sweet 16s, corporate events, and public radio broadcasts.
  • Dirty (Club / Explicit): The full uncut artist delivery. Reserved for 21+ night clubs, private adult birthday celebrations, and festival sets.

2. The 30-Day Cloud Streaming Vault

DJs need to audition new tracks on the go from their iPhones, iPads, or laptops—even when the studio PC is turned off. To achieve this, our engine uploads lightweight, web-optimized audio files into our private Supabase storage bucket (music-vault).

The 30-Day Auto-Purge Policy

To keep our Supabase cloud storage extremely lean (under 1.5 GB total) and prevent storage overload, cloud streaming files have an expiration timestamp set to 30 days from download.

After 30 days, the automated background worker (cloud_stream_purge) purges the audio file from the cloud bucket. Your permanent master audio files on E:\Thunder House - Music are completely safe and untouched!

3. Automated Artist Research & Social Chips

Every track in Thunder Crate includes live, clickable artist intelligence badges:

  • SoundCloud: Direct link to the artist's tracks or verified profile. If an underground artist has no website, their SoundCloud profile is prominently listed.
  • Instagram: Artist IG handle for scouting upcoming tours or aesthetic vibes.
  • Website: Official artist homepage or booking portal.
  • TikTok & YouTube: Visual channels and trending dance challenges.

4. 1-Click High-Quality DJ Downloads

When you are preparing your weekend USB flash drives for Serato DJ Pro or Pioneer Rekordbox, simply click the Download button on the track or within the floating Cyber Audio Dock. The browser downloads the original, tagged MP3 file named perfectly according to DJ software standards: Artist - Title (CLEAN).mp3 or Artist - Title (DIRTY).mp3.
