Chapter 06

Live Gig Execution, Master Timelines & VIP Moment Cues

When it is showtime, the DJ booth relies on admin/gigs.html. This chapter covers how our performers manage minute-by-minute event timelines, execute flawless VIP moment audio cues, and triage live crowd requests in the heat of a performance.

1. The Live Gig Run-of-Show Cockpit

The Run of Show screen transforms all client portal submissions into an actionable, live-performance timeline on the DJ's laptop or tablet:

Zoom In
Figure 6.1 The live Run-of-Show interface at admin/gigs.html. Provides stage-by-stage countdown timers, VIP moment song cues, and live crowd request moderation.

The Anatomy of an Event Timeline:

  1. Prelude & Guest Arrival: Warm, inviting background instrumental music setting the ambiance as family and guests enter the venue.
  2. Ceremony Audio Cues: Exact start cues for grandparents, bridal party processional, and the dramatic pause before the bride's processional.
  3. Cocktail Hour & Mingling: Upbeat, stylish acoustic or soul/R&B vibes while photos are taken.
  4. Grand Introductions: High-energy entrance track with names of bridal party members, parents, and newlyweds pronounced correctly.
  5. Dinner & Background Speeches: Low-volume dining music and crisp wireless microphone handoffs to toast speakers.
  6. VIP Moments: Formal First Dance, Father-Daughter dance, Mother-Son dance, Cake Cutting, and Bouquet Toss.
  7. Dance Floor Open: High-energy party programming, beatmatching, reading the room, and taking the party to peak energy.
  8. Last Dance & Exit: The couple's private final slow dance or high-energy confetti grand exit.

2. Executing VIP Moment Audio Cues

VIP moments are the emotional centerpiece of any wedding or formal banquet. A missed audio cue or playing the wrong edit can ruin a once-in-a-lifetime moment.

The Headphone Audition Rule

Every VIP song has a direct Audition / Stream button built into the gig screen. Before unmuting the master sound system, the DJ must cue the track in their DJ headphones, verify the song title and artist, ensure the volume fader is calibrated, and double-check with the photographer and videographer that cameras are rolling!

3. Triaging Live Crowd Requests

As guests submit song requests through the mobile portal (requests.html), they appear in real-time within the Live Guest Requests Drawer on the DJ's screen:

  • Accept: Adds the song to the DJ's upcoming setlist considerations.
  • Decline: Silently archives the request without interrupting the performance.
  • Played: Marks the request as fulfilled so guests know their favorite anthem was rocked!
  • Automatic Expiration: When the DJ clicks "Mark Gig Completed", the system automatically archives the guest request queue to keep the database lean.
